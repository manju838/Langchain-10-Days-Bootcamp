# Zephyr Airlines Refunds and Changes 2026

## Changing a booking
Economy fares may be changed once for a fee of 1500 rupees plus any fare
difference. Business fares may be changed free of charge up to 24 hours
before departure.

## Cancellations by the passenger
Cancel more than 72 hours before departure and 80 percent of the fare is
refunded. Inside 72 hours, 50 percent is refunded. Inside 4 hours, no refund
is due.

## Cancellations by the airline
If Zephyr Airlines cancels a flight, passengers may choose a full refund or a
free rebooking on the next available service. Refunds are paid within 21
working days to the original payment method.
