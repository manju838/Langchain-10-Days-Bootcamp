# Zephyr Skies Loyalty Programme 2026

## Earning points
Members earn 4 points per 100 rupees spent on Economy fares and 7 points per
100 rupees on Business fares. Points appear within 5 working days of travel.

## Tiers
Silver starts at 10,000 points in a calendar year. Gold starts at 25,000
points. Gold members receive an extra 10 kg of checked baggage and priority
boarding.

## Spending points
Points may be used for fare payment at a rate of 1000 points to 250 rupees.
Points expire 24 months after they are earned.
